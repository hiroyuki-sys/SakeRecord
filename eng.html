1. SakeRecord App Overview

SakeRecord is an iOS-exclusive Japanese Sake management application that allows you to smartly record specs, photos, and taste profiles of the Sake you drink. It features AI-powered label text recognition, shareable tasting card generation, interactive flavor positioning maps with personal preference diagnostics, visual photo albums, and a 47-prefecture conquest tracker.

3. Tab 1: Logs (Sake Record List)

3.1 Main Screen, Record Count, Search, & Filtering

List Display, Tasted / Wishlist Mode Switcher, Record Counter, & Sorting Order

Registered Sake logs are listed in reverse chronological order (newest date first). Mode switcher pills and a live counter are displayed at the top of the list.

  • One-Tap "🍶 Tasted Logs" & "🔖 Wishlist (PRO)" Switcher: Switch instantly between Sake you have already tasted and Sake on your Wishlist (*Pro Plan exclusive). Swipe right on any Wishlist entry and tap "🍶 Drank!" to promote it to a tasted log with today's date.
  • Total & Filtered Record Counter: Displays "登録件数: X件 (Total Records)" at the top of the list, and automatically switches to "検索結果: Y件 / 全X件 (Search Results / Total)" while searching.
  • Brand Name: Positioned on the left (truncated to one line if too long).
  • Tasted Date: Displayed on the right in small gray text.
  • Rating Stars (★): Displayed in orange (★1 to ★5) next to the date (hidden if unrated).
  • Swipe to Delete: Swipe any list row to the left to delete the corresponding log.

Advanced Search Bar (Searchable)

Use the search bar at the top of the screen to instantly filter all saved records.

  • Keyword Search: Performs a fuzzy text search across Brand Name, Specific Designation (Grade), Rice, Yeast, Brewery, Prefecture, and Custom Comments.
  • Direct Star Rating Search: Typing a single half-width digit from "1" to "5" into the search bar instantly filters logs matching that exact star rating (★1 to ★5).

3.2 Toolbar Navigation Buttons

  • Origin Collection Button (🏅 Medal Icon): Located on the left side of the navigation bar. Tapping opens the "Prefecture Conquest Screen".
  • Taste Analysis Map Button (📈 Graph Icon): Located on the left side of the navigation bar. Standard & Pro Plan exclusive. Tapping opens the "Flavor Positioning Map & Preference Diagnosis". Appears disabled (grayed out) in the Free version.
  • Sub-menu Button ("..." Icon):
    • Export Data (square.and.arrow.up): Standard & Pro Plan exclusive. Exports all logs and images as a folder to the iOS Share Sheet.
    • Import Data (square.and.arrow.down): Allows you to select a backup folder to bulk import records into the database.
  • New Entry Button ("+" Icon): Located on the right side of the navigation bar. Tapping brings up the "New Entry Sheet (SakeInputView)" from the bottom.

3.3 New Entry Screen (SakeInputView) Fields & AI Label Scan

This screen is used to log a new Sake entry. It consists of the following input sections:

1. Label & Photo Section + AI Label Scan (Pro Exclusive)

  • Horizontal Scroll Photo Tiles: Displays up to 5 slots from left to right: "Front Label", "Back Label", "Sub 1", "Sub 2", and "Sub 3".
  • Tile Tap Actions: Automatically opens the camera if empty. Opens an enlarged preview gallery if a photo is registered. Tapping "Sub 1–3" in the Free version triggers an upgrade prompt alert.
  • "Register from Album" Button: Select multiple photos at once from your library (up to 2 for Free, up to 5 for Standard/Pro). If a target slot already contains a photo, an "Overwrite Confirmation" alert pops up to prevent accidental deletion.
  • ✨ AI Label Scan (Auto-Extract & One-Tap Apply) *Pro (Expert) Exclusive:
    • Automatically scans attached label photos upon capture/selection (or whenever you tap the "AI Label Scan" button, including on previously saved records).
    • Vertical Text & Kanji Numeral Hybrid Engine: Combines iOS Live Text (VisionKit), a custom per-character vertical column reconstruction algorithm, Scan Dictionary (SakeDictionary) matching, on-device AI (iOS 26 FoundationModels), and optional Gemini Vision API. Recognizes both horizontal and vertical Japanese labels, including Kanji numerals for polishing ratio (e.g., 五十%, 四割五分 = 45%) and alcohol content (e.g., 十六度 = 16%).
    • Non-Destructive Candidate Chips: Displays detected fields as purple chips (e.g., [Grade: Junmai Ginjo], [Rice: Yamadanishiki], [Polishing: 50%], [Alcohol: 16%]). Tap individual chips to apply them, or tap "Apply All" to fill all detected fields at once.
    • Pick from Raw Recognized Text: Expand "Choose from recognized text list" to tap any OCR text line and assign it directly to Brand Name, Brewery, Rice, Style, Yeast, or append to Comments in two taps.

2. Custom Comments Section

A multi-line text editor positioned directly below the photo section. Freely record tasting notes, purchasing store, price, food pairings, and more.

3. Overall Rating Section

Set a rating from 1 to 5 stars (★) by tapping.

4. Basic Specifications Section

  • Brand Name (Required): Enter the Sake brand name.
  • Specific Designation (Grade) (Picker): Select from None / Futsu-shu / Junmai / Tokubetsu Junmai / Junmai Ginjo / Junmai Daiginjo / Honjozo / Tokubetsu Honjozo / Ginjo / Daiginjo / Other.
  • Style (Brewing Process): Kimoto, Yamahai, Muroka Nama Genshu, Fukurodori, Nakadori, Arabashiri, Hi-ire, Namazake, Genshu, Nigorizake, Namazume, Namachozo, Koshu, Sparkling, etc.
  • Rice Type: Yamadanishiki, Gohyakumangoku, Miyamanishiki, Omachi, Dewasansan, Aiyama, etc.
  • Polishing Ratio (%): Entered via numeric keypad (e.g., 50).
  • Alcohol Content (%): Entered via numeric keypad (e.g., 15).
  • Yeast: Kyokai No. 6, No. 7, No. 9, No. 1801, Shizuoka Yeast, House Yeast (Kuratsuki), etc.
  • Brewery: Enter the brewery or sake producer name.
  • Prefecture: Select from all 47 Japanese prefectures + Other.
  • Tasted Date: Select using the date picker (DatePicker).

5. Real-time Predictive Auto-Suggest Bar (Keyboard Toolbar)

When typing in Brand Name, Style, Rice, Yeast, Brewery, or Prefecture, auto-suggest candidates scroll horizontally above the keyboard. Tapping a candidate fills the field and automatically increments its usage count in the custom dictionary.

6. Flavor Analysis Section

  • 4-Axis Flavor Pickers (Segmented Control):
    • Body: Light (1) / Medium (2) / Rich (3)
    • Dryness: Sweet (1) / Medium (2) / Dry (3)
    • Aroma: Subtle (1) / Normal (2) / Expressive (3)
    • Character: Aged (1) / Normal (2) / Fresh (3)
  • Feature Flags (Checkboxes): Effervescent / Rice Umami / Richness / Bitterness / Crisp Finish

7. Record Type (Wishlist) & Serving Temperature / Food Pairings (Pro Exclusive)

  • Record Type (🍶 Tasted / 🔖 Wishlist [PRO]): Save an entry to your Wishlist even with just a photo and no Brand Name entered yet. Wishlist items do not skew your Taste Map or Prefecture Conquest stats until you promote them to "Tasted".
  • Recommended Serving Temperatures [PRO]: Multi-select chips for Chilled (5–10°C), Cool (15°C), Room Temp, Warm (40°C), Hot (50°C+), or On the Rocks / Soda.
  • Best Food Pairings [PRO]: Multi-select chips for Sashimi/Sushi, Grilled Fish, Meat Dishes, Hot Pot/Oden, Cheese/Western, Delicacies, Tempura/Fried, or Solo/Toast—automatically included on Tasting Cards.

3.4 Detail Screen (SakeDetailView), Tasting Card Generator, & Full Viewer (SakeImageDetailView)

View and edit all fields of registered logs, re-scan labels with AI, or generate shareable Tasting Cards.

  • 🍶 Create & Share Tasting Card (SakeTastingCardSheetView): Launch from the orange "Create & Share Tasting Card" button below the photo section or the top-right share icon (square.and.arrow.up).
    • All-in-One Visual Card: Combines the label photo, brand name, brewery & prefecture, date, ★ rating, rice/polishing/alcohol/yeast specs, 4-axis flavor meters, feature tags, and tasting notes into a single refined image.
    • 3 Japanese Color Themes: Switch instantly between "Modern Navy & Gold", "White Porcelain (Kinari)", and "Crimson Lacquer (Akane)".
    • Toggle Personal Notes: Show or hide your custom comments on the card with a single toggle before sharing publicly.
    • Crisp & Lightweight JPEG Export: Renders at 3x Retina resolution while compressing into a lightweight (~200–300KB) JPEG file for sharing via SNS/LINE or saving directly to your iPhone Photo Library.
  • 🔄 Past Repeat & Same-Brewery Comparison Section (Pro Exclusive): Automatically finds and lists past records sharing the same Brand Name (e.g., different vintages or seasonal releases) or the same Brewery at the bottom of the detail screen, showing total repeat count, average ★ rating, and direct navigation links.
  • Brewery Web Search: Tapping the "🔍" icon in the top right opens an in-app browser to search Google for the Brand and Brewery name.
  • Full Viewer, Replacement, Download to Photos, & Deletion (SakeImageDetailView): Tapping any photo tile opens the zoomable full-screen viewer.
    • Replacing Photos (🔄 Camera Icon): Tap the camera replacement icon on the top left to retake or select a new image.
    • Download to iPhone Photos (📥 Download Icon): Tap the download icon (square.and.arrow.down) on the top left to save the currently displayed photo directly to your iPhone's Photo Library.
    • Deleting Photos (🗑️ Trash Icon): Tap the red trash icon (trash) on the top left to show a confirmation dialog and safely delete only the current image.

4. Tab 2: Photo Album (SakePhotoGalleryView) *Standard & Pro Exclusive

Visual Gallery & Pinch Gesture Controls

A dedicated tab available to Standard and Pro (Expert) Plan users. Displays all registered label photos (Front, Back, and Sub) in a clean grid layout (hidden for Free users).

  • Clean Title-free Layout: The navigation header is hidden to maximize viewing area and prevent text overlay on thumbnails.
  • Pinch Column Resizing: Spreading two fingers (pinch-out) enlarges thumbnails (reduces column count down to 2), while pinching together (pinch-in) shrinks thumbnails (increases column count up to 5).
  • Tap Navigation: Tapping any photo tile navigates directly to the detail screen (SakeDetailView) of the corresponding log entry.

5. Tab 3 (Tab 2 in Free Version): Settings & Customization

5.1 Settings Menu Items (SettingsView)

  • Current Plan Display & Switcher: View and switch your active plan tier (Free / Standard / Pro (Expert)).
  • Scan Settings:
    • Edit Scan Dictionary (DictionaryEditView): Add, search, edit, delete, and manage usage counts for dictionary keywords used by both auto-suggest and AI Label Scan.
      • Category switching (Brand / Brewery / Style / Rice / Yeast).
      • Displays "Used X times" badges and "Default" data indicators.
      • Add new keywords and Kana readings via the "+" button.
      • Dictionary Backup & Restore: Export and import SakeDictionary_Backup_yyyyMMdd.json via the top-right "..." menu.
    • Gemini API Key (Pro Exclusive / Optional): While AI Label Scan works offline out-of-the-box using built-in Vision OCR and on-device AI, registering a Gemini API key enables cloud multimodal AI recognition for artistic calligraphy front labels.
  • Help & Tutorials:
    • User Manual: Opens this web manual.
    • Replay Initial Tutorial: Re-displays the onboarding walkthrough slides (SakeTutorialView).
  • About App: Displays the current installed app version.

6. Supplementary Screens & Backup Specifications

6.1 Origin Collection (PrefectureGridView)

Visualizes your conquest status across Japan's 47 prefectures.

  • Conquest Progress Bar: Displays a progress meter at the top reading "X / 47 Prefectures" (counted from tasted logs, excluding Wishlist items).
  • 🗾 Japan Nationwide Block Heatmap & Prefecture Drill-down Sheet (Pro Exclusive): Colors all 47 prefectures on a stylized map of Japan based on how many bottles you have logged. Tapping any prefecture on the map or medal grid opens a sheet listing all Sake you have recorded from that prefecture (with High-Rating / Newest sorting and direct navigation to details).
  • Prefecture Cells: Prefectures with logged Sake display an orange medal (🏅) and count. Unvisited prefectures remain grayed out.

6.2 Interactive Flavor Positioning Map & Preference Diagnosis (SakeTasteMapView) *Standard & Pro Exclusive

Plots your Sake logs onto an interactive 2D map and automatically diagnoses your personal flavor preferences and favorite specs.

  • 2 Map Axis Modes: Switch with one tap between "Dryness × Body" (Sweet↔Dry, Light↔Rich) and "Aroma × Character" (Subtle↔Expressive, Aged↔Fresh).
  • "High-Rated Only (★4+)" Filter: Filter the map to display only your favorite Sake rated 4 or 5 stars.
  • Tap-to-Inspect & Navigate: Tap any dot on the map to pop up a summary card showing the label thumbnail, brand name, grade, rice, brewery, and ★ rating, then tap the card to jump directly to its detail screen. When multiple Sake share the same coordinate area, horizontal selector pills let you inspect every overlapping bottle.
  • Personal Taste Type Diagnosis: Automatically analyzes your high-rated records to generate a personalized taste profile title (e.g., "Expressive Fruity × Rich & Sweet-Umami Type"), description, and top feature hashtags.
  • Top 3 Rankings: Displays your Top 3 Rice Varieties, Top 3 Specific Designations (Grades), and Top 3 Breweries with bottle counts and average ★ ratings.

6.3 Data Backup & Restore Architecture

💡 Log Backup Folder Structure (SakeLog_Backup_yyyyMMdd)
Executing "Export Data" automatically creates a dated folder containing the following files:
  • data.json: Text data, ratings, dates, and settings for all records in ISO8601-compliant JSON.
  • BrandName_8digitUUID_front.jpg / _back.jpg / _sub1.jpg, etc.: Compressed JPEG image files linked to each log.
Executing "Import Data" and selecting this folder safely restores and imports all data without creating duplicate entries.
