1. What is SakeRecord?

SakeRecord is a standalone logging application designed to help you record, organize, and analyze your tasting history and photo collection of Japanese Sake securely on your device (using SwiftData).

2. Initial Tutorial Feature

When you launch the app for the first time, a walkthrough tutorial visually introduces SakeRecord's core highlights (Sake Logging, Photo Collection, and Flavor Map Analysis).

💡 View Anytime:
You can re-open the tutorial at any time by tapping "Show Initial Tutorial" in the Settings tab.

3. Input Screen Details (Add/Edit)

An intuitive layout that allows quick entry even while enjoying a glass of sake.

📷 Label & Additional Photos (Up to 5)

  • Front Label / Back Label / Sub 1-3: 5 dedicated slots for different photos.
  • Batch Import: Select and add up to 5 photos at once from your photo library.

✍️ Custom Notes (Placed directly below photos)

  • Conveniently write tasting notes while visually checking the newly captured or selected label photos right above.

📝 Basic Specifications

  • Record Brand Name, Brewery, and Prefecture (Origin).
  • Sake Grade (Classification): 1-tap picker selection for Junmai, Daiginjo, Tokubetsu Junmai, etc.
  • Style & Rice Type: Record craftsmanship details like "Kimoto" or "Yamadanishiki".
  • Polishing Ratio & Alcohol Degree: Auto-launches number pad with "%" appended automatically.
  • Tasted Date: Accurately select past dates using the DatePicker.

⭐️ Rating & 4-Axis Flavor Analysis

  • Star Rating: Tap stars (1 to 5) for quick evaluation.
  • 4-Axis Sliders: Fine-tune "Body (Light/Full)", "Dryness (Sweet/Dry)", "Aroma (Subtle/Expressive)", and "Character (Aged/Fresh)".
  • Feature Tags (ON/OFF): Quickly toggle tags like "Effervescent", "Rice Umami", "Richness", "Bitterness", and "Crisp Finish".

4. Input History Suggestions & Dictionary Management

SakeRecord features an intelligent built-in dictionary system to minimize repetitive typing.

🔍 Input History Suggestions

  • When focusing on fields like "Brand Name", "Style", "Rice", "Yeast", "Brewery", or "Prefecture", matching keywords from your previous entries or built-in dictionary appear above the keyboard. Tap to instantly fill in the field.

🛠️ Custom Dictionary Management

Manage and maintain saved keywords via "Dictionary Management" in the Settings tab.

  • Categorized View: Browse keywords organized by category (e.g., Brand, Rice).
  • Swipe to Delete: Easily remove typos or unwanted words by swiping left on the item.
  • Usage Counter: Tracks how many times each keyword has been used in your records.

5. Viewing and Managing Sake Details

A beautifully organized screen to review your records with layout order identical to the input screen.

  • Photo Zoom: Tap any photo to enter full-screen view (ZoomableImageView) and pinch out to read fine text on back labels.
  • Inline Editing: Edit details directly from the detail screen with auto-save to SwiftData.

6. How to Use the Photo Gallery Tab

A unique gallery view to visually browse your entire collection.

  • All-in-one Photo Grid: Displays all registered photos in chronological order regardless of brand.
  • Pinch to Resize Grid: Pinch in/out with two fingers to dynamically change grid columns (e.g., enlarge photos with 2 columns, or overview with 5 columns).

7. Flavor Positioning Map Analysis

Automatically analyzes and visualizes your personal taste preferences based on logged data.

  • 2-Axis Visual Map: Plots your recorded sake on a 2D grid with Vertical Axis = "Body (Light/Full)" and Horizontal Axis = "Dryness (Sweet/Dry)".
  • Preference Center: Instantly realize your subtle taste patterns (e.g., "I mostly prefer Light & Dry sake").

8. Data Backup and Restore System

Complete peace of mind with local storage data management.

  • Full Export: Package all text entries and high-resolution photo files into a single backup file.
  • External Storage: Export files safely to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, AirDrop to Mac/PC, or email attachments.
